# OriginFilter — Firefox listing draft (not submitted)

**Name:** OriginFilter

**Short description:** Focus your social feeds with free, local language and evidence-based country filtering for X, Facebook and YouTube.

**Long description:**

OriginFilter gives you controls over what appears in supported desktop social feeds. Choose which creator countries to allow when account-origin evidence is available, hide reliably detected non-English written content, and block individual keywords or account handles.

Features include a country whitelist, a strict mode for unknown-country accounts, a simple pause switch, optional channel-country lookups for YouTube, and a Show once button to reveal hidden items. Most processing is local; optional public YouTube About lookups do not use your login cookies.

Country coverage is limited. A creator's language, name and post location do not prove where they live. Strict unknown-country mode can hide legitimate US accounts, and social networks can change their page layouts. Videos' audio and visual language are not currently inspected.

Filtering is free. You can optionally opt into occasional, clearly labeled OriginFilter-sponsored notices in positions where filtering has hidden content. This preference is OFF by default, removable at any time, and never requires clicking an ad. This build currently contains **no paid sponsor campaigns**. Your browsing history is not sold.

This project is independent and unaffiliated with X, Meta/Facebook and Google/YouTube. For full details, see the bundled privacy notice. Early development build; bug reports welcome.
