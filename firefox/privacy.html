OriginFilter privacy — version 0.6.0

OriginFilter processes supported feed text locally using your browser's language detector. It does not send post text to an AI service. Preferences and cached account-country records remain in local extension storage, not browser sync.

Optional YouTube lookups

When explicitly enabled, the extension requests public channel About pages directly from YouTube, using channel identifiers found in your recommendations. YouTube receives your IP address and the requested channel path. Requests omit login cookies and the referrer. No data is sent to an OriginFilter-operated server. Turning this feature off stops queued requests; a request already sent cannot be recalled.

Local country records

Records contain an account identifier, a country if disclosed, its source, and timestamps. Public YouTube metadata is self-declared, not proof of nationality or current residence. X About disclosures are cached locally when encountered. Missing information is unknown. Facebook manager locations, video geotags, language, names and appearance are not treated as account countries.

Controls and limits

Pause filtering at any time. Clear country records from the popup. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage. Private messages, payment fields, and login forms are not intentionally processed. Feed structures can change, and this development build does not claim universal filtering.

Advertising and payment

This build contains no ads, ad network, payments, analytics, user reporting endpoint, or sale of browsing history. Proposed sponsorship monetization is not active. New data uses require updated disclosures and user consent.

Permissions

Storage saves preferences and country evidence. Access to X/Twitter, Facebook and YouTube enables local feed filtering. Optional Firefox website-content consent permits requested YouTube channel lookups. No access is requested for other websites.
