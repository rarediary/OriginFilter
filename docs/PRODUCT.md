# Product scope — 0.8.0

PC only; Firefox first, Chrome next. X, Facebook, YouTube. Instagram and Android on hold.

Core user preference: English text plus US-based accounts/channels, automatic operation after setup, minimal overhead. Do not claim country where evidence is missing. Language, posting geography, account country and nationality are separate concepts. Do not classify race.

Free local filtering remains the commercial base. Optional, clearly labeled sponsorships are planned, not live. No ad injection, payment collection, analytics or commercial data purchases are authorized by this release.

Country coverage is the principal unsolved launch blocker. Public YouTube channel metadata and encountered X disclosures provide limited evidence; Facebook lacks an automatic provider in this implementation. No universal country filter or server-side feed constraint is claimed. Manual country overrides are personal and not shared as verified facts.

Store launch requires real-browser/live-feed verification, evidence coverage assessment, privacy review, icons/screenshots/listings, signing, developer account authorization and policy review of any future advertising. Do not advertise unavailable automatic origin coverage.

New installations default to a balanced country policy (show unknown); strict country remains optional. The 80% reduction target must be measured on a labeled real-feed sample rather than derived from number of hidden posts. Local per-author language patterns must not be represented as country evidence.
